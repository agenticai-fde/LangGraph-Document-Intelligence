# Cloud Hosting Services Agreement

This Cloud Hosting Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **CloudHost Networks Pvt Ltd** ("Provider") and **ShopEase Retail LLP** ("Customer").

## 1. Term

This Agreement is effective from 1 April 2026 and continues until 31 March 2027. The Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

## 2. Services

The Provider will host the Customer's e-commerce platform on managed cloud servers, including daily backups, 24/7 monitoring and a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment.

## 3. Fees and Payment

The Customer will pay a fixed monthly fee of INR 85,000 plus applicable taxes. Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and are payable within 15 days.

## 4. Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, the Customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for each full 0.5% below the commitment, capped at 50% of the monthly fee.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach with 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured within that period.

## 6. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts of Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.
