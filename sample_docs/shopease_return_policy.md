# ShopEase Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective date: 1 July 2026

## Eligibility

- Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused and in their original packaging with all accessories.
- Opened audio products (earbuds, headphones, speakers) can only be returned if they are defective.
- Gift cards and software licences cannot be returned.

## How refunds work

Once the returned item reaches our warehouse it is inspected within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.

## Damaged or defective items

Report damaged deliveries within 48 hours with photos of the item and packaging. Defective items within the 1-year warranty are replaced free of charge; if a replacement is unavailable, a full refund is issued.

## Return shipping

Return pickup is free for defective, damaged or incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns a fee of Rs 99 is deducted from the refund.
